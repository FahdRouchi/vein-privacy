VEIN – Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 31, 2026

VEIN is a workout-tracking app. This policy explains what happens to your information when you use it.

Summary

VEIN does not collect, sell or share your personal data. Everything you enter stays on your device.

Information stored on your device

To work, the app stores the following only on your phone: your name or nickname, training goal, optional body weight and optional sex setting (used only to estimate strength comparisons), unit preferences, your workouts, personal records, challenge results, appearance settings, and an optional profile photo. We cannot see this information and it is not sent to us or to any server.

Photos and camera

If you choose a profile photo or a background for a share card, the app accesses the photo or camera only after you give permission. The photo is stored on your device and is not uploaded anywhere. You can withdraw permission at any time in your phone's Settings.

Sharing and backups

When you share a record card, a caption, or export a backup, the app hands the file to your device's share sheet. You decide where it goes. A backup file contains your workout data and profile photo; keep it somewhere private.

Analytics, advertising and third parties

VEIN contains no advertising, no analytics and no tracking. We do not sell or share data with third parties.

Strength comparisons

Rankings such as "Top 20%" are estimates calculated on your device from general strength standards. They are for motivation only and are not medical advice.

Children

VEIN does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Deleting your data

Deleting the app removes all data it stored on your device.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this address with a new effective date.
